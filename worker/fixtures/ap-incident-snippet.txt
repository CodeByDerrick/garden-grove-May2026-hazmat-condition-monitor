GARDEN GROVE, Calif. -- Orange County Fire Authority crews remained at GKN Aerospace near 12122 Western Ave after a chemical tank holding methyl methacrylate continued to create concern. Officials said the tank temperature reached 90 degrees Fahrenheit while cooling operations continued and firefighters watched for pressure changes.
